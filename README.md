# New Local Business AI Visibility Checklist

A free, practical checklist for anyone who just opened a local business (a café, salon, plumber, med spa, food truck and so on) and wants to be found on **Google Maps** and recommended by **AI assistants** like ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity.

It's free and open. Copy it, share it, improve it.

---

## Why this matters

AI assistants don't simply read your Google reviews. A [Seeably study](https://x.com/alexgroberman/status/2123456106907480337) checked 6,682 US local businesses on four AI assistants and found:

- **85.6%** were never named by any of the four assistants. That included many businesses with 4.5+ stars and 100+ Google reviews.
- **17.5%** of businesses with a website were named by at least one assistant, against **4.4%** of those without one.
- Of the pages the AI cited, **53%** were lists and articles, **27%** were directories and review sites, and only **20%** were the business's own website.
- Only **4%** of recommended businesses were named by all four assistants. Checking one assistant tells you little about the others.

The takeaway: you need to show up **in the places AI reads**, not only on your own profile.

---

## Week 1: The basics (Google)

- [ ] **Claim and verify your Google Business Profile.** Verification for new businesses can take days, so start on day one.
- [ ] **Pick the most specific primary category.** For example, "Cosmetic dentist" rather than "Dentist", or "Personal injury attorney" rather than "Law firm".
- [ ] Add every **secondary category** that genuinely fits.
- [ ] Write a real **description** of each service: what it is, who it's for, and roughly what it costs.
- [ ] Make sure your **hours, phone, website, address and booking link** are correct and match everywhere.
- [ ] Add **at least 10 real photos**: outside, inside, the team, and your work or products.

## Week 1: The other maps

- [ ] **Apple Business Connect** (businessconnect.apple.com) puts you on Apple Maps and Siri.
- [ ] **Bing Places** (bing.com/forbusiness) puts you on Bing, which some AI tools draw on.
- [ ] **Yelp for Business.** Yelp is one of the sources ChatGPT uses for local recommendations, so claim your page even if you don't plan to advertise.

## Week 2: Directories that AI reads

- [ ] Get listed in the **directories for your industry**, for example Angi, Thumbtack, BBB, Houzz, Healthgrades, Zocdoc, Avvo, OpenTable, TripAdvisor or Vagaro, whichever fit what you do.
- [ ] Make sure your **name, address and phone are identical** on every listing.
- [ ] Look up "**best [your service] in [your city]**" and find the local lists and articles that come up. Ask to be considered, or get mentioned by local press, blogs and neighborhood guides.

## Week 2: Reviews

- [ ] Text every customer from your first weeks and ask for a Google review.
- [ ] Ask them to mention **what you did for them and the neighborhood or city**. That gives AI something specific to quote.
- [ ] Make it a habit: ask every customer within 24 hours. Aim for reviews every week, not a burst once.

## Week 3: Your website

- [ ] **Have a website.** Even a simple one beats none (17.5% vs 4.4% above).
- [ ] Give **each service its own page**, with the service and city in the title.
- [ ] Add a page for each **area you serve**.
- [ ] Answer the questions people ask you on the phone, such as price, timing and what to expect, in an **FAQ**.
- [ ] Add **LocalBusiness schema** (structured data) with your name, address, phone, hours and services.
- [ ] Check your **robots.txt** doesn't block AI crawlers (for example GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot) unless you mean to.
- [ ] Put a **tap-to-call phone number** at the top of the page on mobile.

## Week 4: Check what AI actually says

- [ ] Ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity: "**Who's the best [service] in [city]?**"
- [ ] Ask in a few different ways, including a specific service ("who does same-day water heater repair in [city]?") and a customer need ("where can I get a quiet haircut near [neighborhood]?").
- [ ] Ask: "**What would [your business] need to get recommended here?**" The answer often points to a missing directory, a missing page or too few reviews.
- [ ] Fix what's missing, then check again in a few weeks. Answers vary from run to run, so look for trends, not one result.
